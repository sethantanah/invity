Our Story

A Beautiful Union Under Jehovah's Guidance

For Mr. Yaw Frimpong, life had been a blend of two worlds—spending half his life in the United Kingdom, where he pursued opportunities and experiences, while remaining deeply rooted in his Ghanaian heritage. Despite all that he had achieved, there was one thing he longed for—a companion, a partner with whom he could share the joys and challenges of life under Jehovah's guidance.

During a heart-to-heart conversation with his close friend, Kenneth, Yaw shared his desire to find a woman of virtue and faith—someone whose heart was anchored in Jehovah's principles and who shared his vision of a strong, godly marriage. Kenneth immediately thought of Awurabyna Kwabi-Sarpong—a young woman he had known before his journey to the UK. Raised in a disciplined and God-fearing home, Awurabyna had blossomed into a woman of grace, wisdom, and deep spirituality.

Kenneth, sensing Jehovah's hand in the matter, took it upon himself to reconnect the two. What began as a simple introduction soon turned into something much deeper. Though separated by miles, Yaw and Awurabyna built a strong bond over countless heartfelt conversations. They shared dreams, values, laughter, and faith, discovering in each other a rare and beautiful chemistry. Their connection felt natural, effortless, and divinely orchestrated.

Upon Yaw's return to Ghana, the relationship took on a new dimension. Reunited with the rich flavors of home-cooked meals he had long missed, Yaw found himself embracing not just the familiar comforts of his homeland, but also the warmth and kindness of the woman who would soon become his wife. Each moment spent together reinforced their deep connection, and with every shared experience, their love for each other grew stronger.

Now, as they stand on the threshold of a new chapter, they do so with faith, love, and unwavering trust in Jehovah's guidance. Their relationship is not merely built on emotions, but on shared devotion to Jehovah, with the strong conviction that a threefold cord cannot be quickly torn apart (Ecclesiastes 4:12).

"What Jehovah has yoked together, let no man put apart."
— Mark 10:9
